Drop product photos here and they appear across the site automatically
(catalogue, product pages, Budget Finder, Routine Builder and the homepage
hologram banner).

- **File name** must be the product ID below, e.g. `cerave-hydrating-cleanser.webp`
- **Formats:** .webp (preferred), .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .avif
- **Recommended:** square, at least 800 x 800 px, product centred on a plain
  white or transparent background (white is blended into the tile colour)
- **Rights:** only add images you are licensed to use, e.g. brand press/media
  kits, retailer or affiliate product feeds, or your own photography.
